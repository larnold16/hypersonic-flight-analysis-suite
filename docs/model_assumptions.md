# Model Assumptions

This model is intended for educational and conceptual trajectory analysis. It does not use classified aerodynamic data, flight-test data, real vehicle guidance logic, or validated high-fidelity CFD coefficients. Results should be interpreted as comparative trends rather than flight-certified predictions.

## General Assumptions

- The vehicle is represented as a simplified axisymmetric body or conceptual body preset.
- Aerodynamic coefficients are simplified trend/lookup values, not CFD or wind-tunnel data.
- Atmosphere models are simplified engineering approximations suitable for conceptual studies.
- Thermal, trim, maneuverability, and stability outputs are first-order estimates.
- Launch state and vehicle properties are user-defined conceptual inputs.
- Ground impact terminates trajectory simulations where supported by the stage solver.

## Exclusions

The project intentionally excludes targeting, guidance, interception, weapon employment logic, real vehicle parameters, classified data, and flight-certified validation.
